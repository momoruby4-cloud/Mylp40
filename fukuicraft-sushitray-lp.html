The Maker

Sabae has shaped lacquerware for 1,500 years.

Fukui Craft works out of Sabae — the town at the center of Echizen lacquerware, a craft tradition that traces back over a millennium and a half. This isn't a decorative import; it's the same grade of tableware supplied to Japanese ryokan, sushi counters, and hotel kitchens that can't afford a tray that chips, warps, or looks cheap after a hundred washes.

The stone-like texture on this tray's surface isn't printed — it's a deep, matte lacquer finish designed to hide water spots and knife marks under restaurant lighting, which is exactly why it looks just as good under a kitchen pendant at home.
